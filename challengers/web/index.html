    Questions
    
    
    What is ChaLLengErs?
    ChaLLengErs (Challengers CV) is a free AI CV builder and job-application toolkit for jobs in Europe. Paste a job ad and it tailors your CV to it, scores it against ATS rules and exports PDF and Word files.
    

    Is ChaLLengErs really free?
    Yes. There is no subscription, no sign-up and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser and your data stays on your device.
    

    Will my CV pass ATS systems?
    ChaLLengErs builds single-column, text-based CVs with standard headings and checks keyword match, structure, action verbs, numbers and length. No tool can guarantee a pass on every ATS, but these are the rules ATS parsers and recruiters rely on.
    

    Which countries does it support?
    Finland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, the Netherlands, Belgium, France, Ireland, the United Kingdom, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Poland and Estonia, plus a general Europass-style format.
    

    Can I use it with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?
    Yes. ChaLLengErs writes a master prompt you paste into any AI chat, then you paste the answer back to get a formatted, scored CV.
    

    Is ChaLLengErs an AI Apply alternative?
    Yes. It covers CV tailoring, cover letters, interview practice, CV translation, LinkedIn import, job search and form autofill for free, without bots that submit applications for you.
